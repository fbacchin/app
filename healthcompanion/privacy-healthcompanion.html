Privacy Policy

Your Health Companion AI — for iPhone and Apple Watch

Effective date: 25 July 2026

Overview

Your Health Companion AI ("the App") is developed by Fabrizio Bacchin. The App is designed with privacy at its core: your personal data stays on your device. We do not run advertising, we do not use third-party analytics or tracking, and we never sell your data.

Data stored on your device

Everything you create in the App — your profile (name, age, sex, height, weight, goals), tracked activities and GPS routes, gym workouts, food diary, and coaching insights — is stored locally on your iPhone in the App's private database. It is not transmitted to us or to any third party.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your explicit permission, the App reads data from Apple Health (such as steps, heart rate, active energy, activity rings and body weight) and writes data to it (workouts, active energy and body weight).

  • Health data is used solely to display your metrics and generate on-device insights.
  • Health data never leaves your device and is never shared with, sold to, or disclosed to third parties.
  • Health data is never used for advertising, marketing, or data-mining purposes.
  • You can review or revoke access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Location

The App uses GPS only while you record an outdoor activity, to draw your route and compute distance and pace. Routes are stored locally on your device (and, if you allow it, in Apple Health as workout routes). Your location is never sent to our servers or to third parties.

Camera

The camera is used exclusively to scan food barcodes. No photos are captured, stored, or transmitted.

Food database (Open Food Facts)

When you search for a food or scan a barcode, the App sends the search text or barcode number to Open Food Facts, a public, non-profit food database, to retrieve nutrition information. These requests contain no personal identifiers and are not linked to your identity or your health data.

Future features: accounts and sync

Future versions of the App may offer an optional account and cloud synchronisation (hosted in the European Union). If introduced, these features will be strictly opt-in, this policy will be updated beforehand, and the in-app privacy questionnaire will reflect the change.

Data retention and deletion

  • You can erase all App data at any time from Profile → "Delete all data".
  • Uninstalling the App removes its local database from your device.
  • Data written to Apple Health remains under your control in the Health app.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Not medical advice

The App provides general wellness information and is not a medical device. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified professional for medical or nutritional guidance.

Your rights (GDPR)

Because your data is stored only on your device, you exercise your rights of access, rectification and erasure directly within the App. For any privacy question or request, contact us at bacchin@mac.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new effective date.
