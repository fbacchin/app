Frequently asked questions

My activity rings are empty. Open Profile → "Connect the Health app" and grant the requested permissions. On the iPhone, rings reflect the data recorded by Apple Health.

GPS route is missing. Make sure the App has location permission (Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services) and start the activity outdoors.

A food is missing from the database. Food search and barcodes are powered by Open Food Facts. You can always add a food manually with its calories and macros.

How do I delete my data? Profile → "Delete all data" removes everything stored by the App on your device.
