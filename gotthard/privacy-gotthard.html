Gottardo Live — last updated 8 September 2026

1. Who we are

Gottardo Live is an iOS app by Fabrizio Bacchin (Switzerland) that shows real-time traffic information for the Gotthard road tunnel. For anything related to this policy, contact bacchin@tiscali.it.

2. Advertising

The free app shows one banner advert supplied by Google AdMob. To select, deliver, measure and protect those ads, Google's advertising SDK may process technical data from your device — such as a device/advertising identifier, IP address, coarse (non-precise) location and ad-interaction data. For this processing Google acts as an independent data controller under its own policies.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options inside the app. If you decline, only non-personalised (“limited”) ads are shown.

The app asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any tracking. If you allow it, ads may be personalised using your device's advertising identifier (IDFA); if you decline, you will only see non-personalised ads. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Apple's SKAdNetwork is used for privacy-preserving install measurement.

3. Data the app sends (push notifications)

If — and only if — you allow notifications, the app registers your device with our notification service (Back4App, a Parse hosting provider). The registration contains:

  • the anonymous Apple push token of your device (APNs)
  • a random installation identifier generated by the app
  • device type ("ios"), app version, time zone and language setting

None of this identifies you personally, it is not linked to any account and it is used exclusively to deliver traffic notifications. It is never sold or shared for advertising. If you deny or later disable notifications, no registration is created or used. You may request deletion of your registration at any time by email.

4. Services the app talks to

To display live data and ads, the app connects to the services below. As with any internet request, they technically process your IP address for the duration of the request.

4a. Purchases (removing the advertising)

The app sells one thing: hiding the banner. We never see your payment details. The purchase is made and charged by Apple, and no card number, name or billing address ever reaches us.

Because the app has no user account, something still has to remember that you bought it — otherwise a new phone would show ads again. That job is done by RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), which receives:

  • a random anonymous identifier generated by the app — not your name, e-mail or Apple Account
  • the receipt Apple issues for the purchase, which says what was bought and, for the monthly subscription, when it expires
  • the app version, the platform and the country of your App Store

That is all it is used for: answering the single question may this device hide the advert? It is not used for advertising and is not sold. If you never buy anything, nothing about a purchase is ever sent.

5. Usage statistics (anonymous)

To learn which parts of the app are actually used, and how often the app is left without data from its source, the app sends a small number of anonymous events to PostHog (PostHog Inc.), hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt). Each event is a fixed name — screen viewed, direction detail opened, webcam opened, chart range changed, data unavailable, tour completed, notification permission checked, store unavailable — with at most one fixed value, such as which screen or which webcam.

  • No location, no identifiers of ours, no alert text, no queue or waiting-time figures, no free text
  • No screen recording: the app does not capture what is on your display
  • PostHog groups the events of one installation under a random identifier that is not linked to your name, e-mail or any account, and is not used to track you across other apps or websites
  • As with any internet request, PostHog technically processes your IP address for the duration of the request

We use these statistics only to improve the app. The complete list of events lives in the app's source code; if a new one is ever added, this page changes with it.

6. Data stored on your device only

Your settings, your ad-consent choice and the local traffic-history samples used for the 24-hour chart are stored on your device and are not uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

7. What we don't do

  • No user accounts, no profiles
  • No usage measurement beyond the anonymous events described in section 5 — no screen recording, no profiles
  • No tracking without your permission — we ask through Apple's App Tracking Transparency and honour your choice
  • No sale of your data
  • No data knowingly collected from children — the app is a traffic utility rated 4+

8. Your rights

Under Swiss data-protection law (nFADP) and the GDPR you may request access to, correction or deletion of the data described in section 3. Since we hold no identifying information, please include the approximate date you enabled notifications so we can locate the registration. Write to bacchin@tiscali.it. For the advertising data processed by Google, please refer to Google and Google's own privacy controls.

9. Changes

This policy will be updated whenever the app's data practices change, and the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.

Vos réglages, votre choix de consentement publicitaire et les échantillons locaux de l'historique utilisés pour le graphique sur 24 heures sont stockés sur votre appareil et ne nous sont pas transmis. La suppression de l'app les efface.

7. Ce que nous ne faisons pas

  • Aucun compte utilisateur, aucun profil
  • Aucune mesure d'usage au-delà des événements anonymes décrits à la section 5 — aucun enregistrement d'écran, aucun profil
  • Aucun suivi sans votre autorisation — nous la demandons via App Tracking Transparency et respectons votre choix
  • Aucune vente de vos données
  • Aucune donnée sciemment collectée auprès d'enfants — c'est un outil de trafic classé 4+

8. Vos droits

En vertu de la loi suisse sur la protection des données (nLPD) et du RGPD, vous pouvez demander l'accès, la rectification ou la suppression des données décrites à la section 3.
