In breve

Mortgage Calculator e Mortgage Calculator Light sono app di Fabrizio Bacchin (Svizzera) e questa informativa vale per entrambe. Le app non raccolgono dati personali: nessun account, nessun profilo, nessuna vendita di dati. Raccolgono soltanto statistiche d'uso anonime — quali schede vengono aperte e quali funzioni usate, mai i numeri che inserisci — descritte per intero più sotto. I dati del mutuo che inserisci restano sul tuo dispositivo (per le notifiche push, se le attivi, vengono registrati solo dati tecnici anonimi — vedi sotto). Mortgage Calculator non contiene alcuna pubblicità. Mortgage Calculator Light, la versione gratuita, mostra annunci di Google AdMob e, in Europa, ti chiede il consenso prima di richiedere anche un solo annuncio.

Le app conservano localmente, nel proprio contenitore sandbox, soltanto i parametri del mutuo che inserisci — importo, data di inizio, tasso di interesse e durata — così da ritrovarli al riavvio e da poterli condividere fra le schede Calcolo, Piano di ammortamento e Scenari. In short

Mortgage Calculator and Mortgage Calculator Light are apps by Fabrizio Bacchin (Switzerland) and this policy covers both. The apps collect no personal data: no account, no profile, no data selling. They do collect anonymous usage statistics — which tabs are opened and which features used, never the figures you enter — described in full below. The mortgage figures you enter stay on your device (for push notifications, if you enable them, only anonymous technical data is registered — see below). Mortgage Calculator contains no advertising at all. Mortgage Calculator Light, the free version, shows Google AdMob ads and, in Europe, asks for your consent before requesting a single ad.

Data stored on your device

The apps store locally, inside their own sandbox container, only the mortgage parameters you enter — amount, start date, interest rate and duration — so they can be restored on relaunch and shared between the Calculation, Amortization plan and Scenarios tabs. Mortgage Calculator Light also stores your advertising consent choices there.

All calculations — payment, amortization plan, scenarios — happen entirely on the device: no data is sent to any server, and there is no cloud synchronisation.

Exporting the amortization plan

From the Amortization plan tab you can export the schedule as CSV or PDF. The file is generated on the device and handed to the iOS share sheet: you decide whether to save, print or send it, and to whom. The apps never upload or transmit these files anywhere.

Usage statistics

Both apps measure how they are used, to understand which features genuinely help and which do not. The service is PostHog, hosted on its European servers (eu.i.posthog.com). Collection is anonymous: there is no account, no profile, and nothing that can be traced back to you.

The apps send only these seven signals, and nothing else:

There is no screen recording. These statistics are not tracking and do not use the IDFA: tracking concerns only advertising in Mortgage Calculator Light, described below.

Push notifications

If you choose to enable notifications, the app registers a few technical items with Back4App (a Parse platform):

  • the device APNs token, generated by Apple, required to deliver notifications;
  • an anonymous installation identifier, used to manage deliveries;
  • basic technical data such as device type, app version and time zone.

This data is not linked to your identity and is used solely to make notifications work. If you decline or later turn notifications off in Settings, the token becomes unusable; you may request its deletion by writing to the contact below.

Advertising — Mortgage Calculator Light only

Mortgage Calculator Light is free thanks to advertising — a banner in the Calculation, Amortization plan and Scenarios screens, plus occasional full-screen ads — served by Google AdMob. To select and measure ads, Google may process:

  • technical identifiers of the device and, only if you allowed tracking, the IDFA;
  • your IP address (which indicates an approximate location) and technical device information;
  • ad interaction data (impressions, taps).

How Google processes this data is described in the Google privacy policy and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services". Mortgage Calculator contains none of this.

Your consent — Mortgage Calculator Light only

On first launch, if you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the app shows Google's consent form: you can choose between personalised ads and generic ads not based on your interests. No ad is requested before you have made your choice. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time from the app's App info tab, under Privacy settings → Manage consent.

Right after Google's form, the app shows Apple's system prompt (App Tracking Transparency) and asks whether you allow tracking. These are two distinct questions: the first is about European consent, the second about your iPhone's advertising identifier (IDFA).

  • If you allow it, the IDFA becomes available to Google and ads can be personalised. In that case tracking for advertising purposes does take place, which is exactly why permission is asked.
  • If you refuse, or do not answer, iOS withholds the IDFA and ads stay generic. The app forces nothing and uses no alternative identifier to work around your choice.

You can change your mind at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

The app works exactly the same whichever choice you make: only the ads change, never the features.

What we don't do

  • No account and no user profile
  • No screen recording, in either app
  • No tracking in Mortgage Calculator, and none in Mortgage Calculator Light unless you allow it
  • No collection of the figures you enter: amounts, payments, rates and terms never leave the device
  • No access to your location: the apps never request it
  • No advertising in the paid version
  • No selling or commercial sharing of your data

Your rights and contact

Under the GDPR and the Swiss data protection act (nFADP) you have the right to withdraw consent at any time (see above), to request access to or deletion of the technical data described in the notifications section, and to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. For any question about this policy write to bacchin@tiscali.it. To exercise your rights over data processed by Google, refer to the channels indicated in Google's policy.

  • los identificadores técnicos del dispositivo y, solo si has autorizado el seguimiento, el IDFA;
  • la dirección IP (que indica una ubicación aproximada) e información técnica del dispositivo;
  • datos de interacción con los anuncios (impresiones, toques).

Cómo trata Google estos datos se describe en la política de privacidad de Google y en la página «Cómo usa Google los datos». Mortgage Calculator no contiene nada de esto.

Tu consentimiento — solo Mortgage Calculator Light

En el primer inicio, si te encuentras en el Espacio Económico Europeo o en el Reino Unido, la app muestra el formulario de consentimiento de Google: puedes elegir entre anuncios personalizados y anuncios genéricos, no basados en tus intereses. No se solicita ningún anuncio antes de que hayas elegido. Puedes cambiar o retirar tu elección en cualquier momento desde la pestaña Info app, en Ajustes de privacidad → Gestionar consentimiento.

Justo después del formulario de Google, la app muestra el aviso del sistema de Apple (App Tracking Transparency) y te pregunta si autorizas el seguimiento. Son dos preguntas distintas: la primera se refiere al consentimiento europeo, la segunda al identificador publicitario (IDFA) de tu iPhone.

  • Si lo autorizas, el IDFA queda disponible para Google y los anuncios pueden personalizarse. En ese caso sí hay seguimiento con fines publicitarios, y es justamente por eso que se pide permiso.
  • Si lo rechazas, o no respondes, iOS no facilita el IDFA: los anuncios siguen siendo genéricos. La app no fuerza nada ni usa ningún identificador alternativo para eludir tu elección.

Puedes cambiar de idea en cualquier momento en Ajustes → Privacidad y seguridad → Seguimiento.

La app funciona igual sea cual sea tu elección: solo cambian los anuncios, no las funciones.

En bref

Mortgage Calculator et Mortgage Calculator Light sont des apps de Fabrizio Bacchin (Suisse) et cette politique vaut pour les deux. Les apps ne collectent aucune donnée personnelle : pas de compte, pas de profil, aucune vente de données. Elles ne collectent que des statistiques d'usage anonymes — quels onglets sont ouverts et quelles fonctions sont utilisées, jamais les chiffres que vous saisissez — décrites en détail plus bas. Données conservées sur votre appareil

Les apps conservent localement, dans leur propre conteneur isolé, uniquement les paramètres du prêt que vous saisissez — montant, date de début, taux d'intérêt et durée — afin de les retrouver au redémarrage et de les partager entre les onglets Calcul, Tableau d'amortissement et Scénarios. Mortgage Calculator Light y enregistre aussi vos choix de consentement à la publicité.

Tous les calculs — mensualité, tableau d'amortissement, scénarios — se font entièrement sur l'appareil : aucune donnée n'est envoyée à un serveur et il n'existe aucune synchronisation dans le cloud.

Export du tableau d'amortissement

Depuis l'onglet Tableau d'amortissement, vous pouvez exporter le tableau au format CSV ou PDF. Le fichier est généré sur l'appareil et remis à la feuille de partage iOS : c'est vous qui décidez de l'enregistrer, de l'imprimer ou de l'envoyer, et à qui. Les apps ne téléversent ni ne transmettent ces fichiers nulle part.
