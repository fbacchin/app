Politique de confidentialité

Valichi Live — last updated 9 August 2026

1. Who we are

Valichi Live is an iOS app by Fabrizio Bacchin (Switzerland) that shows official waiting times at European border crossings and Alpine corridors. For anything related to this policy, contact bacchin@tiscali.it.

2. In-app purchases

Regional packs and the "All of Europe" pack are one-time in-app purchases processed entirely by Apple. We never see your name, payment details or billing address. The app only receives an anonymous confirmation from Apple's StoreKit that a pack is unlocked, and stores that entitlement on your device and in your Apple Account (so it can be restored on your other devices). See Apple's privacy policy.

3. Advertising (free version)

The free version shows one banner advert supplied by Google AdMob. To select, deliver, measure and protect those ads, Google's advertising SDK may process technical data from your device — such as a device/advertising identifier, IP address, coarse (non-precise) location and ad-interaction data. For this processing Google acts as an independent data controller under its own policies.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and iOS asks separately — through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt — whether the app may track you. If you decline either, the app works exactly the same and ads are simply not personalised. Any purchase removes ads entirely, and with them all advertising data processing.

4. Data the app sends (push notifications)

If — and only if — you allow notifications, the app registers your device with our notification service (Back4App, a Parse hosting provider). The registration contains:

  • the anonymous Apple push token of your device (APNs)
  • a random installation identifier generated by the app
  • the list of crossing channels you follow (e.g. "valico-hr-me-karasovici"), device type ("ios"), app version, time zone and language setting

The followed channels reveal only which crossings you want alerts for — nothing identifies you personally, nothing is linked to any account, and nothing is used for advertising. If you deny or later disable notifications, no registration is created or used. You may request deletion of your registration at any time by email.

5. Services the app talks to

The waiting times of all crossings are collected by our server-side collector from the national authorities and republished as open data on GitHub: your device never contacts the authorities' websites, it only reads our aggregated files. As with any internet request, the services below technically process your IP address for the duration of the request.

ServicePurpose
GitHub Pages / raw.githubusercontent.comaggregated waiting times, 48-hour history, crossing catalogue
Apple StoreKitin-app purchases and restore (see section 2)
Back4Apppush-notification delivery (see section 4)
Google AdMobadvertising in the free version only — see Google's privacy policy

6. Data stored on your device only

Your settings, followed crossings, ad-consent choice and cached traffic data are stored on your device and are not uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

7. What we don't do

  • No user accounts, no profiles
  • No analytics or usage measurement of our own
  • No location tracking — the app never asks for your position
  • No tracking without your permission, no sale of your data
  • No data knowingly collected from children — the app is a traffic utility rated 4+

8. Your rights

Under Swiss data-protection law (nFADP) and the GDPR you may request access to, correction or deletion of the data described in section 4. Since we hold no identifying information, please include the approximate date you enabled notifications so we can locate the registration. Write to bacchin@tiscali.it. For advertising data processed by Google, please refer to Google; for purchase data, to Apple.

9. Changes

This policy will be updated whenever the app's data practices change, and the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.

Se rifiuti, l'app funziona esattamente allo stesso modo e gli annunci semplicemente non sono personalizzati. I canali seguiti rivelano solo per quali valichi vuoi gli avvisi — nulla ti identifica personalmente, nulla è collegato a un account e nulla è usato per la pubblicità. Le attese di tutti i valichi sono raccolte dal nostro collector lato server presso le autorità nazionali e ripubblicate come open data su GitHub: il tuo dispositivo non contatta mai i siti delle autorità, legge solo i nostri file aggregati.
